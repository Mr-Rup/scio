    omnistats Documentation

    omnistats is an autonomous statistical inference engine, one-tap exploratory data analysis (EDA) suite, multivariate regression toolkit, and pure numerical hypothesis testing framework for Python (>= 3.11).

    Architectural Layers

    omnistats enforces a strict 5-layer downward dependency graph:

    1. omnistats.core (L0): Immutable result containers (TestResult, RegressionResult, EDAReport, ScreenReport, SVGFigure), typed domain exceptions (StatError, SampleSizeError, ZeroVarError), 8-role semantic column classification (Role), universal DataFrame coercion (ensure_pandas_df), and HTML/Rich/LaTeX/Markdown/APA/SVG renderers.
    2. omnistats.functional as F (L1): Stateless, deterministic NumPy/SciPy mathematical kernels returning lightweight NamedTuples (TTestResult, ANOVAResult, RMAnovaResult, NonParametricResult, TOSTResult, CorrelationResult, BayesFactorResult).
    3. omnistats.models (L2): Wilkinson formula design matrix parser (parse_formula), Ordinary Least Squares (ols with QR decomposition and MacKinnon-White HC0–HC3 robust standard errors), Type II & Type III Factorial ANOVA (anova_table), Newton-Raphson IRLS binary logistic regression (logistic), Poisson log-linear GLM (poisson with QMLE HC3 SEs & IRR), and Linear Mixed-Effects Models (mixedlm via Profiled REML/ML).
    4. omnistats.stat & omnistats.eda (L3): Autonomous assumption-checked decision router (compare, screen, tost, solve_power) and vectorized exploratory data analysis (read, profile, audit_quality, correlations, vif).
    5. omnistats.integrations & omnistats.cli (L4): Native Pandas (df.scio) and Polars (pl_df.scio) DataFrame accessors, dual-backend statistical plotting (plot_raincloud, .plot()), and the omnistats command-line interface.

    Feature Matrix

    Capability High-Level API (sc.* / df.scio.*) Stateless Kernel (omnistats.functional as F)
    Two-Sample Parametric sc.compare(df, x="arm", y="val") F.student_t, F.welch_t, F.paired_t, F.one_sample_t
    Two-Sample Non-Parametric Auto-routed on normality/pathology failure F.mann_whitney, F.wilcoxon, F.permutation_test
    K-Group Omnibus & Post-Hoc Auto-triggers Tukey, Games-Howell, or Dunn F.anova, F.welch_anova, F.kruskal, F.tukey, F.games_howell, F.dunn
    Repeated Measures & Paired sc.compare(df, x="cond", y="val", paired=True) F.rm_anova (Greenhouse-Geisser), F.friedman, F.kendalls_w, F.mcnemar
    Factorial ANOVA / ANCOVA sc.anova_table(df, "y ~ C(A) * C(B)", typ=2) omnistats.models.anova_table (Type II & Type III SS)
    Linear, GLM, & Mixed Models sc.ols, sc.logistic, sc.poisson, sc.mixedlm QR HC0–HC3 & Newey-West HAC OLS, IRLS Logistic/Poisson, Profiled REML LMM
    Survival Analysis sc.coxph(df, "Surv(time, event) ~ x1 + x2") F.kaplan_meier (Greenwood log-log CI), F.logrank_test, Harrell's \(C\)-index
    Causal Inference sc.ate(df, y, treat, covars), sc.covariate_balance Doubly Robust AIPW, Hajek IPTW, Standardized Mean Difference (SMD)
    Time-Series Diagnostics sc.ols(df, "y ~ x", robust_se="HAC") F.adf_test (Unit Root), F.ljung_box (\(Q\)-test), F.durbin_watson
    Effect Sizes & Exact CIs Populates .effect and .effect_ci F.cohen_d_ci, F.hedges_g_ci, F.cliffs_d_ci, F.eta_sq, F.omega_sq
    Correlation & Partial Corr sc.compare(df, x="num1", y="num2") F.pearson_r, F.spearman_r, F.kendall_tau, F.partial_corr
    Bayesian & Power Analysis sc.solve_power(effect=0.5, power=0.8) F.bayes_factor_t (JZS \(BF_{10}\)), F.interpret_bf, F.solve_power_t
    Resampling & Equivalence sc.tost(a, b, bound=0.5) F.bootstrap_ci (\(BC_a\)), F.permutation_test, F.tost
    Robust Statistics Outlier-resistant location & inference F.trimmed_mean, F.winsorized_mean, F.huber_location, F.yuen_t
    Visualization & Polars result.plot(), sc.plot_raincloud, pl_df.scio omnistats[plot] (matplotlib + SVGFigure), omnistats[polars]
